International placement

Truck Drivers – Lithuania

JobBridge International is inviting applications from African nationals with truck-driving experience who are interested in relocating to Lithuania for employment.

Who can apply
  • African nationals willing to migrate and work in Lithuania.
  • Applicants with a valid heavy-vehicle driving licence appropriate to the role.
  • Truck-driving experience and a responsible approach to road safety.
  • Applicants willing to complete the required recruitment, documentation and assessment process.
Migration cost support

For candidates selected for placement, the company will cater for migration-associated costs. The covered items, arrangements and timing will be explained to selected candidates before they make a commitment.

Submitting an application does not guarantee selection, a job offer, a visa or migration approval. All placements and immigration processes are subject to the relevant employer and government requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Useful information before you apply.

How do I apply for a vacancy?

Review the advertised requirements and contact the placements team using the application link provided with the vacancy.

Does submitting an application guarantee a job?

No. Applications are subject to screening, employer selection, any required assessments and relevant approvals.

Where can I ask for more information?

Email placements@jobbridgeinternational.online and include the vacancy title in your message.
